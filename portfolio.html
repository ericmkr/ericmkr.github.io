Mobile app UX/UI Design — FlowTrack

Mobile task management application with intuitive interface and engaging micro-interactions.

Problem

Users find productivity apps too complex and abandon them after a few days of use.

Approach

In-depth user research, simplified information architecture, micro-interactions to encourage daily engagement, iterative testing with real users.

Results

  • Prototype tested — 45% increase in retention in the first week.
  • Minimalist interface with smooth onboarding and consistent animations.
  • User satisfaction rating: 4.8/5 stars.

Tools : Miro, Visily, Google Apps Script, Gamma.

Low-fidelity mockup for an advertising agency website — World Wide Wall

A low-fidelity mockup illustrating the structure and key features of the World Wide Wall advertising agency website, focusing on layout, navigation, and the presentation of core services.

Problem

World Wide Wall lacks a clear and user-friendly website structure, making it difficult for visitors to understand its services and engage with the agency. A low-fidelity mockup is needed to define a coherent layout and improve the overall user experience.

Approach

The project begins with a discovery and research phase aimed at understanding user needs, business goals, and the competitive landscape, allowing us to identify what visitors expect from the World Wide Wall website. Based on these insights, we will establish a clear information architecture that organizes content logically, defines navigation flows, and prioritizes essential information to ensure intuitive browsing.

Tools : Miro, Visily.
